# Business rules

- Do not collapse two unknown customers into one merely because wording matches.

- Do not fabricate quotes, percentages, revenue impact, or statistical confidence.

- Preserve low-frequency but material failures; volume alone is not priority.

- A source theme label is data to review, not a proven explanation of the problem.

## Difficult example

Identical anonymous reviews have no identity evidence. Keep both and report total unique customers as unknown.

## Review evidence

Check deduplication, quote/source accuracy, theme counts, and evidence behind suggested fixes. Do not infer causation or representative market sentiment from a biased sample.
