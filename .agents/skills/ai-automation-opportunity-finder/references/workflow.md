# Business rules

- A high-volume task can still be a poor pilot if data access or review costs dominate.

- No evidence means assumption-only, not a calibrated confidence score.

- Savings rank uses hours, not mixed currencies.

- Do not turn an exploration request into authorization to connect or change company systems.

## Difficult example

A short task needs more review time than the proposed automation saves. Keep the negative result; do not promote it as a win.

## Review evidence

Test with business operators on real, redacted process samples. Check whether the output changes a practical decision and whether the pilot brief is implementable.
