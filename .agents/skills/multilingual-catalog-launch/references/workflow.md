# Business rules

- This skill starts from an approved catalog; catalog creation belongs to a separate workflow.

- Structural checks do not certify linguistic quality or localized legal claims.

- Use a review ledger keyed by SKU, locale, source revision, reviewer, and decision.

- Do not overwrite source copy or treat an older translation as current after a revision mismatch.

## Difficult example

The source product changed from revision r1 to r2. Mark the r1 translation stale even when its syntax is valid.

## Review evidence

Use native-reviewed samples; test product facts, placeholders, variant IDs, and import columns. Existing translation suites are a serious baseline for comparison.
