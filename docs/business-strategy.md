# Business strategy

The repository offers small working versions of business solutions DEVS AI can
implement more deeply. A free package completes a useful job from files. The natural
service opportunity is connecting that job to live data, team approvals, and ongoing operation.

Start with catalog preparation, order support, and company knowledge Q&A. The portfolio
then expands into document intake, product finders, repeatable SOPs, quoting, localization,
and customer feedback. Prefer recurring operational work with a named business owner.

## Where DEVS AI knowledge belongs

- Exact source/variant/unit checks and review decisions.
- Tricky examples that ordinary prompts miss.
- Usable import files and working artifacts, not only recommendations.
- Rules for preserving unknowns, contradictions, and exceptions.
- Evidence that a workflow reduced correction effort on a reviewed example.

Do not claim a workflow is unique merely because its name is new. Existing business
plugins and commerce/localization tools cover nearby jobs. Differentiate with useful
scope, examples, business rules, and measured outcomes.

## Promotion and contact

The website/README can identify DEVS AI and offer relevant implementation help.
The skill must remain usable without contact, signup, telemetry, or uploading data to us.
Do not inject advertisements into support replies, quotes, or customer-facing copy.

Success is useful work followed by qualified implementation enquiries and won work.
Installation counts and developer attention alone do not establish this objective.
Query themes in metadata are hypotheses, not verified search volumes.
